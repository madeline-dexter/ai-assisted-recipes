# Green Chile Stew with Nopales

**Servings:** 2 generous servings

## Ingredients

- 16 oz fresh nopales, cleaned and cut into bite-size pieces
- 3 Hatch/New Mexican green chiles
- 2 small serrano peppers
- ½ small yellow onion, halved
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- 2 tbsp neutral oil, divided
- Kosher salt, to taste

## Instructions

1. Heat 1 tbsp of the oil in a large saucepan or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add the nopales and sauté for 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until most of the mucilage has cooked away and the nopales are tender with some browned spots. Transfer to a bowl.

2. Wipe out the pan if necessary. Heat it dry over medium-high heat, with no oil. Add the Hatch chiles, serrano peppers, and onion. Char, turning occasionally, until the chiles are blistered and deeply blackened and the onion is deeply charred and softened. Work in batches if necessary.

3. Transfer the Hatch chiles to a bowl and cover for 5 minutes. Peel away the skins, remove the stems and seeds, and roughly chop. Remove the stems from the serranos. Peel away any badly blackened outer layers from the onion and roughly chop.

4. Add the remaining 1 tbsp oil to the saucepan over medium heat. Add the charred onion and serranos and cook for 2–3 minutes.

5. Add the charred onion, serranos, and ½ cup of the broth to a blender. Blend until mostly smooth. Return the mixture to the saucepan and cook for 5–8 minutes, stirring frequently, until slightly darker, thicker, and fragrant.

6. Add the chopped Hatch chiles and remaining 1½ cups broth. Season with kosher salt to taste. Stir in the sautéed nopales.

7. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook uncovered for 30–45 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the Hatch chiles have softened and dispersed into the stew and the broth has taken on a cohesive green-chile character.

8. If the stew becomes too thick, add a little more broth. Taste and adjust the salt before serving.
