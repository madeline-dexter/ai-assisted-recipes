# Rasam Egg-Drop Soup with Okra & Tomato

**Servings:** 4 bowls

## Ingredients

### Okra
- 12–16 small okra pods, thinly sliced
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- Kosher salt, to taste

### Rasam Broth
- 4 cups low-sodium vegetable broth or water
- 3–4 small tomatoes, finely chopped
- 2 tsp rasam powder
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- ½ tsp ground turmeric
- 2 garlic cloves, finely minced
- 1–2 green chiles, thinly sliced
- 2–3 tsp tamarind concentrate, to taste
- Kosher salt, to taste

### Egg Drop
- 4 large eggs, beaten

### Thickener
- 2 tsp cornstarch
- 2 tbsp cold water

### Tempering
- 2 tsp neutral oil
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 2 dried red chiles
- 10–12 curry leaves, if available

### Finishing
- Fresh cilantro, chopped
- Freshly ground black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. **Brown the okra.** Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large skillet over medium-high to high heat. Add sliced okra in as close to a single layer as possible. Season lightly with salt and cook 6–8 min, stirring occasionally, until well browned and most characteristic sliminess has cooked away. Transfer to a plate.

2. **Make tempering.** Heat 2 tsp oil in a saucepan over medium-high. Add mustard seeds and cumin seeds. When popping, add dried red chiles and curry leaves. Cook 20–30 sec.

3. **Build rasam base.** Add garlic and green chile; cook 30 sec. Add tomatoes, turmeric, rasam powder, cumin, black pepper, and a pinch of salt. Cook 3–4 min until tomatoes soften.

4. **Make broth.** Add broth and 2 tsp tamarind concentrate. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook uncovered 7–10 min. Taste and adjust with more tamarind, salt, or black pepper. Broth should be distinctly tangy and peppery but savory, not concentrated tamarind.

5. **Return okra.** Add browned okra and simmer 1–2 min to heat through.

6. **Thicken.** Whisk cornstarch with 2 tbsp cold water until smooth. Slowly stir the slurry into the simmering soup. Simmer about 1 min until lightly glossy and thick enough to coat a spoon. If too thin, add a little more slurry; if too thick, thin with broth or water.

7. **Egg ribbons.** Reduce heat to low, keeping the soup at a gentle simmer rather than a boil. Slowly drizzle the beaten eggs in a thin stream while stirring gently in one direction. Once the egg forms ribbons, stop stirring and let set for 30–60 sec.

8. **Finish.** Turn off the heat. Taste and adjust salt, tamarind, and black pepper. Finish generously with chopped cilantro.

**Important Note:** For the intended American-Chinese egg-drop-soup texture, brown the okra separately until most of the mucilage cooks away. Cornstarch—not okra—is responsible for the silky thickness.
