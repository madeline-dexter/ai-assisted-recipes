# Campanelle with Fig Butter & Ricotta

**Servings:** 1–2

## Ingredients

- 6 oz campanelle
- 2 tbsp fig butter, plus more to taste
- 2 tbsp plant ricotta or sour cream
- 1–2 tsp finely grated lemon zest
- 1–2 tbsp pasta water, as needed
- 1 tbsp toasted sliced or slivered almonds
- Tiny pinch of freshly grated nutmeg
- Small pinch of salt

## Steps

1. Bring a pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the campanelle #according to the package directions until al dente. Reserve about ¼ cup of pasta water before draining.
2. While the pasta cooks, gently toast the almonds in a dry skillet over medium-low heat until lightly golden and fragrant. Remove from the skillet and set aside.
3. Return the drained pasta to the warm skillet over low heat. Add the fig butter, lemon zest, and 1 tablespoon of pasta water. Toss gently until the fig butter loosens and coats the pasta.
4. Remove the skillet from the heat. Add the vegan ricotta or tofu-based vegan sour cream and nutmeg. Toss gently until creamy, adding another splash of pasta water if needed to create a silky sauce.
5. Taste and add a small pinch of salt if needed.
6. Transfer to a bowl and finish with the toasted almonds.
