# Red Wheat & Rye Salad with Date Vinaigrette

**Servings:** 8

## Ingredients

### Salad
- 2 cups cooked hard red winter wheat berries
- 2 cups cooked rye berries
- 3 medium cucumbers, diced
- 1½ cups finely chopped fresh parsley
- 1 cup finely sliced red onion
- 1 cup toasted walnuts, chopped

### Dressing
- 6 tbsp olive oil
- 4 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 2 tbsp white wine vinegar
- 1½ tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp black pepper

### For Serving
- Date syrup, to taste

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the grains.** Make sure the cooked wheat and rye berries are completely cooled. If refrigerated, fluff them with a fork.
2. **Combine the salad.** Add the wheat, rye, cucumber, parsley, red onion, and walnuts to a large bowl. Toss gently.
3. **Dress the salad.** Add the olive oil, lemon juice, white wine vinegar, salt, and black pepper to the salad. Toss thoroughly and, optionally, refrigerate for 30–60 minutes to allow the ingredients to marinate.
4. **Finish and serve.** Divide the salad among individual bowls and drizzle each serving with date syrup to taste.
