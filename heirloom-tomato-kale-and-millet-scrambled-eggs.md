# Heirloom Tomato, Kale & Toasted Millet Scrambled Eggs

**Servings:** 1–2

## Ingredients

### Millet
- ¼ cup dry millet
- ½ cup water
- Pinch of salt

### Scrambled Eggs
- 4 large eggs
- 1 heirloom tomato, chopped
- 1 cup chopped kale
- 2 tablespoons sour cream
- 1 teaspoon mustard
- 1 small clove black garlic, finely minced
- Salt, to taste
- Black pepper, to taste
- 1 tablespoon butter or oil, for cooking

### Optional
- Nutritional yeast, to taste
- Hot sauce or crushed red pepper

## Instructions

1. **Toast the millet:** Place the dry millet in a dry skillet over medium heat. Toast for several minutes, stirring frequently, until fragrant and lightly golden.

2. **Cook the millet:** Add ½ cup water and a pinch of salt. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook until the water is absorbed and the millet is tender, about 15 minutes. Remove from heat and let sit covered for 5 minutes, then fluff.

3. **Prepare the eggs:** Whisk the eggs with the sour cream, mustard, black garlic, salt, and black pepper until well combined.

4. **Cook the vegetables:** Heat the butter or oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the chopped kale and cook until it begins to soften. Add the heirloom tomato and cook briefly, just until warmed and slightly softened.

5. **Add the millet:** Stir in the cooked toasted millet and cook for another minute or two so everything is hot and well combined.

6. **Scramble the eggs:** Lower the heat to medium-low. Pour in the egg mixture and gently stir and fold until the eggs are softly set and creamy. Remove from the heat while they are still slightly moist.

7. **Finish:** Taste and adjust the salt and pepper. Add nutritional yeast or hot sauce/crushed red pepper if desired.

**Important Note:** The toasted millet gives the eggs a pleasantly nutty, hearty texture. Avoid overcooking the eggs—the mixture should remain soft and creamy rather than dry.
