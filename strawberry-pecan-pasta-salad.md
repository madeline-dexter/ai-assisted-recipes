# Strawberry-Pecan Pasta Salad

**Servings:** 2

## Ingredients

- 4 oz (about 2 cups) dry fusilli
- 1 cup chopped fresh strawberries
- ¾ cup coconut strawberry yogurt
- ¼ cup chopped pecans, toasted
- Drizzle of honey, to finish

## Instructions

1. Cook the fusilli according to the package directions until just tender. Drain and rinse under cold water until cooled. Drain well.
2. Add the cooled fusilli, strawberries, and coconut strawberry yogurt to a bowl. Toss gently until evenly coated.
3. Fold in the toasted pecans.
4. Chill until ready to serve.
5. Finish with a drizzle of honey.
