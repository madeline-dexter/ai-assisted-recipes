# Strawberry-Pecan Pasta Salad

**Servings:** 2

## Ingredients

- 4 oz (about 2 cups) dry fusilli
- 1 cup chopped fresh strawberries
- ¾ cup strawberry coconut yogurt
- ¼ cup chopped pecans, toasted
- Drizzle of honey, to finish

## Instructions

1. **Cook the fusilli.** Cook the fusilli according to the package directions until just tender. Drain and rinse under cold water until cooled. Drain well.

2. **Toss the salad.** Add the cooled fusilli, strawberries, and coconut strawberry yogurt to a bowl. Toss gently until evenly coated.

3. **Add the pecans.** Fold in the toasted pecans.

4. **Chill the salad.** Chill until ready to serve.

5. **Finish and serve.** Drizzle with honey just before serving.
