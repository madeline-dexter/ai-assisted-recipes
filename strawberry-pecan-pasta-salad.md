# Strawberry-Pecan Pasta Salad

**Servings:** 2

## Ingredients

- 4 oz (about 2 cups) dry fusilli
- 1 cup chopped fresh strawberries
- ¾ cup coconut strawberry yogurt
- 1 tbsp light brown sugar
- ¼ cup chopped pecans, toasted

## Instructions

1. Cook the fusilli according to the package directions until just tender. Drain and rinse under cold water until cooled, then drain well.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together the coconut strawberry yogurt and brown sugar until smooth.
3. Add the cooled fusilli and chopped strawberries. Toss gently until evenly coated.
4. Fold in the toasted pecans.
5. Serve chilled.
