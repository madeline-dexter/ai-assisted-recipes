# Pearl Onion & Wild Rice Stew

**Servings:** 2 generous

## Ingredients

- 1 cup frozen pearl onions
- ½ cup wild rice blend, rinsed
- 1½ cups vegetable broth
- 1 small carrot, finely diced
- 1 celery stalk, finely diced
- 2 tbsp plant butter, divided
- 2–3 dashes Maggi seasoning
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- ¼ tsp ground coriander
- ½ tsp dried thyme
- 1 bay leaf
- 2–3 tsp apple cider vinegar
- Kosher salt, to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- Plant-based sour cream, for finishing

## Instructions

1. Melt 1 tbsp plant butter in a saucepan over **medium-low heat**. Add the carrot, celery, Maggi seasoning, and a pinch of kosher salt. Cook for **7–10 minutes**, stirring occasionally, until the vegetables are soft, glossy, and beginning to sweeten but not browned.
2. Add the remaining 1 tbsp plant butter and the frozen pearl onions. Increase the heat to **medium**. Cook for **5–8 minutes**, stirring occasionally, until the onions have thawed, their released moisture has cooked off, and they begin to brown in the butter.
3. Reduce the heat to **medium-low**. Add the tomato paste, coriander, and thyme. Cook for **1–2 minutes**, stirring constantly, until the tomato paste darkens slightly and the spices are fragrant.
4. Stir in the wild rice blend and bay leaf. Add the **1½ cups vegetable broth** and bring to a gentle boil.
5. Reduce the heat to **low**, cover partially, and simmer according to the wild rice blend’s package directions, usually about **35–50 minutes**, until the rice is tender and the stew reaches a hearty, spoonable consistency. Add a splash of water if needed.
6. Remove the bay leaf. Stir in **apple cider vinegar to taste** and season with **freshly ground black pepper to taste**. Adjust seasoning as needed.
7. Serve with **a dollop of plant-based sour cream**.
