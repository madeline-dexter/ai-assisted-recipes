# Vermont-Style Apple Pie Mac & Cheese

**Servings:** 2

## Ingredients

- 6 oz small elbow macaroni or cavatelli
- ½ cup apple pie filling, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp plant butter
- 1 tbsp all-purpose flour
- ¾ cup plant milk of choice
- 1 cup sharp plant-based cheddar shreds
- ¼ tsp Dijon mustard
- ¼ tsp freshly ground black pepper
- Salt, to taste
- 2 slices plant-based cheddar cheese, for serving

## Instructions

1. Cook the pasta in salted water until just al dente. Reserve ¼ cup pasta water, then drain.
2. Melt the plant butter in a saucepan over medium-low heat. Whisk in the flour and cook for 1 minute.
3. Gradually whisk in the plant milk. Cook, whisking, until slightly thickened, about 2–3 minutes.
4. Reduce the heat to low and stir in the cheddar shreds, Dijon mustard, and black pepper. Stir until smooth.
5. Stir in the chopped apple pie filling and cook for about 1 minute, just until warmed through.
6. Add the pasta and toss until evenly coated. If the sauce is too thick, add a splash of reserved pasta water. Taste and adjust the salt.
7. Divide between bowls.
8. Place a slice of plant-based cheddar cheese over each serving and lightly torch the surface until just melted and lightly browned.
