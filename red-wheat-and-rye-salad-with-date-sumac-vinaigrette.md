# Red Wheat & Rye Salad with Date-Sumac Vinaigrette

Servings: 4

## Ingredients

### Salad
- 1½ cups cooked hard red winter wheat berries
- ¾ cup cooked rye berries
- 1½ medium cucumbers, diced
- ¾ cup finely chopped fresh parsley
- ½ cup finely sliced red onion
- ¾ cup pomegranate arils
- ½ cup toasted walnuts, chopped

### Date-Sumac Vinaigrette
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 2 tsp white wine vinegar
- 1½–2 tsp date syrup
- 1¼ tsp ground sumac
- Salt, to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the grains.** Make sure the cooked wheat and rye berries are completely cooled. If refrigerated, fluff them with a fork.
2. **Combine the salad.** Add the wheat, rye, cucumber, parsley, red onion, pomegranate, and walnuts to a large bowl. Toss gently.
3. **Make the vinaigrette.** Whisk together the olive oil, lemon juice, white wine vinegar, date syrup, sumac, salt, and black pepper until well combined.
4. **Keep separate.** Refrigerate the salad and vinaigrette separately until serving to prevent the grains from absorbing the dressing and the cucumber from becoming overly soft.
5. **Dress before serving.** Shake or whisk the vinaigrette again, drizzle it over the salad, and toss gently. Adjust the salt, sumac, lemon juice, vinegar, or date syrup as desired.

**Important note:** Keeping the vinaigrette separate preserves the chewy texture of the wheat and rye and the freshness of the cucumber. Add it shortly before serving.
