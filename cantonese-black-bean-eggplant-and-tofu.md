# Cantonese Black Bean Eggplant & Tofu

**Servings:** 2

## Ingredients

### Five-Spice Bulgur
- ¼ cup dry bulgur
- ½ cup vegetable broth
- ¼ tsp Chinese five-spice
- Pinch of salt

### Tofu & Eggplant
- 14 oz nigari firm tofu, cut into small cubes
- 1 cup canned eggplant, drained, with some of its oil reserved
- 1 large egg, lightly scrambled

### Sauce
- 2 tbsp black bean sauce
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp Shaoxing cooking wine
- 1 tsp sugar
- ½ cup vegetable broth
- ½ tsp cornstarch

### Aromatics
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- 1–2 fresh red chiles, thinly sliced
- 2 scallions, sliced
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil

## Instructions

1. **Cook the bulgur.** Combine the bulgur, vegetable broth, five-spice, and salt in a small saucepan. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook until the bulgur is tender and the liquid is absorbed. Fluff with a fork.

2. **Add the tofu.** Gently stir-fry the tofu in some of the reserved eggplant oil for 1–2 minutes, allowing some pieces to break apart.

3. **Cook the aromatics.** Add the garlic, ginger, and chiles and stir-fry for about 30 seconds.

4. **Add the eggplant and bulgur.** Add the canned eggplant and cooked bulgur. Stir-fry gently for 1–2 minutes.

5. **Make the sauce.** Stir together the black bean sauce, soy sauce, Shaoxing cooking wine, sugar, broth, and cornstarch. Pour into the skillet and bring to a gentle simmer.

6. **Braise the tofu.** Simmer for 3–4 minutes, stirring gently, until the sauce thickens slightly and coats everything.

7. **Add the egg.** Fold in the lightly scrambled egg and cook for another 30–60 seconds, until heated through and evenly distributed.

8. **Finish and serve.** Remove from the heat and stir in the sesame oil. Scatter with scallions and serve hot over rice.
