# Hong Kong Pumpkin-Corn Chowder

Servings: 1 generous serving

## Ingredients

- ½ cup canned pumpkin purée
- ½ cup canned creamed corn
- 1¼–1½ cups vegetable broth
- 1 egg, beaten
- 1–2 green onions, thinly sliced
- ½ tsp grated fresh ginger
- 1 tsp neutral oil
- 1–2 tsp sriracha, to taste
- ¼ tsp white pepper
- ⅛ tsp mushroom powder (optional)
- 1 tsp Chinese black vinegar, plus more to taste
- Salt, to taste

## Instructions

1. **Cook the ginger.** Warm the oil in a small saucepan over medium-low heat. Add the ginger and cook for 30–60 seconds, stirring until fragrant.

2. **Bloom the seasonings.** Stir in the white pepper and optional mushroom powder; cook for about 15 seconds.

3. **Build the chowder.** Stir in the pumpkin, creamed corn, and 1¼ cups vegetable broth until smooth.

4. **Simmer gently.** Cook for 6–8 minutes, stirring frequently, until hot. Add more broth, up to 1½ cups total, to reach your preferred consistency.

5. **Make the egg ribbons.** Reduce the heat to a gentle simmer. Slowly drizzle in the beaten egg while stirring the soup in one direction. Stop stirring and let the ribbons set for 30–60 seconds.

6. **Finish with vinegar and sriracha.** Remove from the heat and stir in the Chinese black vinegar and 1 tsp sriracha. Taste and add more vinegar or sriracha as desired.

7. **Season and serve.** Add salt to taste and finish with the sliced green onions.
