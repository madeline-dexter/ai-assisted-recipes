# Green Čorba with Trahana & Sorrel

Servings: 2 generous servings

## Ingredients

### Soup
- 3 cups vegetable broth
- ¼ cup Krajina-style trahana
- ½ cup cooked white beans
- ½ small onion, finely diced
- ½ medium carrot, finely diced
- 2 tbsp vegan butter
- ¼ tsp ground coriander
- ½ tsp freshly ground black pepper
- ½ cup well-drained pickled sorrel, finely chopped
- 1–2 tsp pickling liquid from the sorrel
- Salt, as needed

### To Finish
- ½ cup vegan sour cream

## Steps

1. **Build the butter base.** Melt 1½ tbsp of the vegan butter in a small saucepan over medium-low heat. Add the onion and carrot and cook for 7–9 minutes, stirring occasionally, until very soft and sweet but not browned.

2. **Bloom the coriander.** Stir in the ground coriander and cook for 20–30 seconds, just until fragrant.

3. **Build the čorba.** Add the vegetable broth, trahana, white beans, and black pepper. Bring to a gentle boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for 8–12 minutes, until the trahana is tender and the soup has thickened slightly.

4. **Add the sorrel.** Stir in the chopped pickled sorrel and 1–2 tsp of its pickling liquid. Simmer for 2–3 minutes. Taste and adjust the salt.

5. **Finish with butter.** Stir in the remaining ½ tbsp vegan butter and let it melt completely into the hot soup.

6. **Finish with sour cream.** Remove the soup from the heat. Stir a few spoonfuls of hot broth into the vegan sour cream to loosen it, then stir the tempered sour cream back into the soup. Do not boil after adding it.

7. **Serve.** Serve hot.
