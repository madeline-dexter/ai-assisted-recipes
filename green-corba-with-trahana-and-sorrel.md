# Green Čorba with Trahana & Sorrel

Servings: 2 generous servings

## Ingredients

### Soup
- 3½ cups vegetable broth
- ⅓ cup Krajina-style trahana
- 1 (15-oz) can white beans, drained and rinsed
- ¾ small onion, finely diced
- 1 medium carrot, finely diced
- 2½ tbsp plant butter
- ⅓ tsp ground coriander
- ½ cup pickled sorrel

### To Finish
- ½ cup plant-based sour cream
- ½ tsp freshly ground black pepper
- Kosher salt, to taste

## Steps

1. **Build the butter base.** Melt 2 tbsp of the plant butter in a small saucepan over medium-low heat. Add the onion and carrot and cook for 7–9 minutes, stirring occasionally, until very soft and sweet but not browned. Stir in the ground coriander and cook for 20–30 seconds, just until fragrant.

2. **Build the čorba.** Add the vegetable broth, trahana, and white beans. Bring to a gentle boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for 8–12 minutes, until the trahana is tender and the soup has thickened slightly.

3. **Add the sorrel.** Stir in the chopped pickled sorrel along with any liquid clinging to it. Simmer for 2–3 minutes.

4. **Finish with butter.** Stir in the remaining ½ tbsp plant butter and let it melt completely into the hot soup.

5. **Finish with sour cream, pepper, and salt.** Remove the soup from the heat. Stir a few spoonfuls of hot broth into the plant-based sour cream to loosen it, then stir the tempered sour cream back into the soup. Stir in the freshly ground black pepper and kosher salt to taste. Do not boil after adding the sour cream.
