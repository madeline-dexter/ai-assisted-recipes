
# Zucchini-Carrot Caviar Pasta Salad

**Servings:** 4

## Ingredients

**Pasta salad**
- 12 oz dry small cavatappi
- 20 oz zucchini-carrot caviar (kabachkovaya ikra)
- ⅓ cup mayonnaise

**Dressing & seasoning**
- 4 tsp apple cider vinegar
- ½ tsp ground coriander
- ¼ tsp ground black pepper
- ⅛ tsp ground mustard
- 2 tbsp finely chopped fresh dill (optional)
- Salt to taste

## Instructions

1. **Cook the pasta.** Cook cavatappi in salted water until al dente. Drain, rinse briefly under cold water, and drain thoroughly.
2. **Make the dressing.** Whisk together the mayonnaise, apple cider vinegar, ground coriander, black pepper, and ground mustard.
3. **Mix in the ikra.** Fold the zucchini-carrot caviar into the dressing. Taste before adding salt, since the ikra may already be seasoned.
4. **Combine the salad.** Toss the cooled pasta with the ikra mixture. Fold in the fresh dill, if using.
5. **Chill and adjust.** Refrigerate for 30 minutes. Taste and adjust the vinegar, mayonnaise, and seasoning as needed.
