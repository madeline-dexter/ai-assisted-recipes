# Doenjang Stroganoff with Mushrooms & Tofu

**Servings:** 2

## Ingredients

### Stroganoff
- 4 cups (about ½ lb) dry egg noodles
- 8 oz cremini or baby bella mushrooms, thinly sliced
- ½ small white onion, finely diced
- 1 large garlic clove, finely minced
- 3 tbsp doenjang
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 2–3 dashes Maggi seasoning
- ½ tsp ground black pepper
- ½ cup vegetable stock
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
- 1 tbsp sunflower or other neutral oil

### Fried Tofu
- 6 oz extra-firm tofu, pressed and cut into ¾-inch cubes
- 1 tbsp all-purpose flour
- ¼ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp ground black pepper
- 1 tbsp sunflower or other neutral oil

### Finishing
- 2 tbsp plant-based sour cream
- 2 tbsp finely chopped fresh chives

## Instructions

1. **Cook the noodles.** Cook the egg noodles according to the package directions. Reserve ½ cup of the hot cooking water before draining.

2. **Dust the tofu.** Toss the tofu with the flour, salt, and ground black pepper until lightly and evenly coated.

3. **Fry the tofu.** Heat the oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add the tofu and cook, turning occasionally, until browned and firm on several sides, about 6–8 minutes. Transfer to a plate.

4. **Brown the onion and mushrooms.** Wipe out any excess flour or browned residue from the skillet. Heat the oil over medium-high heat. Add the onion and stir-fry for about 1 minute, until very lightly browned. Add the mushrooms and stir-fry for 2 minutes, until lightly browned while still firm.

5. **Fry the doenjang.** Add the doenjang and stir-fry for 1–2 minutes, coating the onion and mushrooms and lightly toasting the doenjang in the moisture released by the mushrooms.

6. **Add the garlic and deglaze.** Add the garlic and cook for about 30 seconds. Pour in the vegetable stock and scrape up any browned doenjang from the bottom of the skillet.

7. **Build the sauce.** Stir in the Dijon mustard, toasted sesame oil, Maggi seasoning, and ground black pepper. Simmer until the sauce is slightly thickened.

8. **Add the noodles.** Add the drained noodles and toss until thoroughly coated. Add a splash of reserved noodle cooking water as needed to loosen the sauce.

9. **Finish and serve.** Divide between two bowls and top with the fried tofu, plant-based sour cream, fresh chives, and toasted sesame seeds.
