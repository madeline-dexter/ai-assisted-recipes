# Whipped Giant Beans with Amarena Cherries

**Servings:** 2

## Ingredients

### Whipped Giant Beans
- 1½ cups cooked giant white beans
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 tablespoons sour cream
- ½ teaspoon ground coriander
- ¼ teaspoon white pepper
- ⅛ teaspoon nutmeg
- Salt to taste

### Toppings
- 2 tablespoons slivered almonds
- 5–6 Amarena cherries, finely chopped
- 2–3 teaspoons Amarena cherry syrup

## Instructions

1. **Whip the beans.** Blend the giant white beans with the olive oil and sour cream until smooth and creamy, adding a splash of water as needed to reach a light, whipped consistency.

2. **Season the beans.** Stir in the coriander, white pepper, nutmeg, and salt.

3. **Toast the almonds.** Toast the slivered almonds in a dry skillet over medium-low heat until lightly golden and fragrant. Set aside.

4. **Plate the beans.** Spoon the whipped beans onto plates and smooth into a thick, creamy layer.

5. **Finish with the toppings.** Top with the toasted slivered almonds and chopped Amarena cherries, then drizzle with the Amarena cherry syrup.
