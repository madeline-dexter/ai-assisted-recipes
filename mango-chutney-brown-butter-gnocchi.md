# Mango Chutney Brown Butter Gnocchi

**Servings:** 2

## Ingredients

- 16 oz gnocchi
- 4 tbsp oat or other plant-based butter
- 3–4 tbsp mango chutney
- 5–6 fresh sage leaves, plus a few extra for garnish
- 3 fresh thyme sprigs
- 2 small garlic cloves, minced (optional)
- 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 2 tsp vinegar or lemon juice, to taste
- Salt, to taste
- Chili flakes, optional
- Oil, as needed

## Instructions

1. **Fry the gnocchi.** Heat a little oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the gnocchi in a single layer, working in batches if necessary, and fry for 6–10 minutes, turning occasionally, until golden and crisp. Transfer to a plate.

2. **Toast the butter.** Add the plant-based butter to the skillet over medium-low heat. Heat until melted, fragrant, and lightly toasted, allowing it to darken slightly if the formulation permits. Avoid overheating or burning the oils.

3. **Infuse the herbs.** Add the sage leaves and thyme sprigs, reserving a few sage leaves for garnish. Cook for 20–30 seconds until fragrant. If using garlic, add it and cook for another 20 seconds.

4. **Make the sauce.** Reduce the heat to low. Stir in the mango chutney, black pepper, and 2 tbsp water. Let the sauce bubble gently for 30–60 seconds, then remove the sage leaves and thyme sprigs.

5. **Balance and toss.** Stir in the vinegar or lemon juice to taste. Return the gnocchi to the skillet and toss until coated. Season with salt and chili flakes, if desired. Serve immediately, garnished with the reserved sage leaves.
