# Purple Sweet Potato–White Miso Soup

**Servings:** 2 generous servings

## Ingredients

### Soup
- 4–5 small purple sweet potatoes, peeled and cut into chunks
- 1–1½ cups vegetable stock
- ½ small onion, diced
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1 Tbsp vegan butter or olive oil
- ½ tsp ground coriander
- ¼ tsp freshly ground black or white pepper
- 1–2 Tbsp white miso
- 2–3 Tbsp vegan sour cream
- 1–2 tsp fresh lemon juice, or to taste

### Topping
- 2 Tbsp slivered almonds
- A small drizzle of olive oil or melted vegan butter
- Freshly ground black pepper

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the sweet potatoes.** Peel and cut the purple sweet potatoes into chunks.

2. **Sauté the aromatics.** Heat the vegan butter or olive oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Add the onion and cook until softened, about 4–5 minutes. Add the garlic and coriander and cook for about 30 seconds.

3. **Cook the sweet potatoes.** Add the sweet potatoes and vegetable stock. Bring to a simmer and cook until the potatoes are completely tender, about 15–20 minutes.

4. **Blend.** Using an immersion blender, blend the soup directly in the saucepan until completely smooth. Add a little more stock if necessary to reach a thick but pourable, velvety consistency.

5. **Add the miso.** Remove the soup from the heat. Mix the miso with a few spoonfuls of hot soup in a small bowl until smooth, then stir it back into the pot.

6. **Make it creamy.** Stir in the vegan sour cream.

7. **Add lemon.** Stir in 1 tsp lemon juice, taste, and add more as desired. The lemon should brighten the soup without making it distinctly lemony.

8. **Toast the almonds.** Place the slivered almonds in a dry skillet over medium-low heat. Toast, stirring frequently, until golden and fragrant. Remove from the pan immediately.

9. **Serve.** Ladle the soup into bowls. Top with the toasted slivered almonds, a small drizzle of olive oil or melted vegan butter, and freshly ground black pepper.

**Important note:** Add the lemon after removing the soup from direct heat to preserve its flavor. Start with less lemon and increase gradually—the acidity is there to balance the sweet potato and miso, not dominate the soup.
