Sichuan Cold Lupini Bean Salad

Servings: 3–4

Ingredients

* 2 cups prepared lupini beans, drained and rinsed
* ½ cup roasted, shelled peanuts
* 2 scallions, thinly sliced
* 2 tbsp fermented soybean chili oil, including fermented soybeans
* 1 tbsp Chinkiang vinegar
* 1 tsp green Sichuan peppercorn oil
* 1 small garlic clove, finely minced
* fresh cilantro, chopped, to taste

Instructions

1. Prepare the dressing. Combine fermented soybean chili oil, Chinkiang vinegar, green Sichuan peppercorn oil, and minced garlic.
2. Marinate the beans. Toss lupini beans with the dressing until evenly coated. Refrigerate for 20–30 minutes.
3. Add the peanuts. Fold in the roasted peanuts.
4. Finish the salad. Add scallions and cilantro. Toss gently and serve chilled or at cool room temperature.
