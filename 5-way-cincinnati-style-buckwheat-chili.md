# 5-Way Cincinnati-Style Buckwheat Chili

**Servings:** 4

## Ingredients

### Chili

- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- 1 small yellow onion, very finely minced
- 2 small serranos, very finely minced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 Tbsp tomato paste
- 1 (10 oz) can Rotel diced tomatoes and chilies
- 1½ cups vegetable broth
- 1 Tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 1½ oz unsweetened baking chocolate, or 1 Tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1½ tsp ground chili powder
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp sweet paprika
- ¾ tsp ground cinnamon
- ¼ tsp ground allspice
- ¼ tsp ground cloves
- ¼ tsp ground coriander
- ⅛ tsp ground cayenne pepper
- ¼ tsp ground white pepper
- 1 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste

### Buckwheat

- ¼ cup dry buckwheat groats
- ½ cup water
- Pinch of salt

### For serving

- 12 oz spaghetti
- ½ small yellow onion, very finely diced
- 1 (15 oz) can kidney beans, drained, rinsed, and warmed
- Plant-based cheddar cheese shreds
- Plant-based sour cream
- Oyster crackers

## Instructions

1. **Cook the onion and serranos.** Heat the olive oil in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Add the onion and serranos and cook for **5–7 minutes**, stirring occasionally, until softened and lightly browned.

2. **Add the garlic and tomato paste.** Stir in the garlic and cook for 30 seconds. Add the tomato paste and cook for **1–2 minutes**, stirring frequently, until slightly darkened.

3. **Toast the spices.** Stir in the chili powder, cumin, sweet paprika, cinnamon, allspice, cloves, coriander, cayenne, and white pepper. Cook for about **30 seconds**, until fragrant.

4. **Build the chili.** Stir in the baking chocolate or cocoa powder, then add the Rotel, vegetable broth, and apple cider vinegar. Bring to a simmer.

5. **Simmer.** Reduce the heat to low and simmer uncovered or partially covered for **20–25 minutes**, stirring occasionally. The tomatoes should break down somewhat and the chili should remain loose and finely textured. Add a little water or broth if necessary.

6. **Finish the chili.** Taste and adjust the salt, vinegar, and cayenne if needed.

7. **Cook the buckwheat.** Rinse the buckwheat. Combine it with the water and salt in a small saucepan. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook for about **10–12 minutes**, until tender but still distinctly chewy and al dente. Drain any excess water and fluff.

8. **Cook the spaghetti.** Cook the spaghetti according to the package directions. Drain well.

9. **Assemble the 5-way.** Divide the spaghetti among four bowls. Spoon the chili over the spaghetti, then add a modest amount of al-dente buckwheat. Top each serving with kidney beans, finely diced raw onion, plant-based cheddar cheese shreds, and a dollop of plant-based sour cream. Serve with oyster crackers.
