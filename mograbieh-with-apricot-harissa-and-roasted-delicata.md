# Mograbieh with Apricot Harissa & Roasted Delicata

**Servings:** 2

## Ingredients

### Mograbieh
- 1½ cups cooked mograbieh
- Salt, to taste

### Apricot Harissa
- 2 tbsp apricot jam
- 1 tsp harissa
- 1–2 tsp fresh lemon juice
- 1 tsp olive oil

### Roasted Delicata
- 1 delicata squash, halved lengthwise, seeded, and sliced into half-moons
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Kosher salt, to taste

### Fried Shallots
- 1 large shallot, very thinly sliced
- Neutral oil, for frying
- Kosher salt, to taste

### Fried Sage
- 4–6 fresh sage leaves
- 1 tbsp olive oil

### Finishing
- Fresh cilantro, chopped

## Instructions

1. **Roast the delicata.** Heat oven to 425°F. Toss the delicata with olive oil and salt. Arrange in a single layer and roast for 20–25 min, flipping halfway through, until tender and deeply browned at the edges.

2. **Make the apricot harissa.** Stir together the apricot jam, harissa, lemon juice, and olive oil. Taste and adjust the harissa or lemon juice as needed.

3. **Cook the mograbieh.** Cook the mograbieh according to package directions. Drain well and season lightly with salt.

4. **Fry the shallots.** Heat about ½ inch of neutral oil in a small skillet over medium heat. Add the sliced shallot and fry, stirring occasionally, until deeply golden and crisp. Transfer to a paper towel and season lightly with salt.

5. **Fry the sage.** Heat the olive oil in a small skillet over medium heat. Add the sage leaves and fry until crisp, about 20–30 sec per side. Transfer to a paper towel.

6. **Combine.** Toss the mograbieh with the apricot harissa until evenly coated, adding a splash of water if needed to loosen the sauce.

7. **Plate.** Spoon the mograbieh onto plates and arrange the roasted delicata over the top. Scatter with the fried shallots and fried sage, then finish with chopped cilantro.
