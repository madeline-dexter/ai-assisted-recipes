# Rødgrød with Creamy Farina

**Servings:** 2

## Creamy Farina

### Ingredients
- 1 cup regular oat milk
- ¼ cup farina
- 1 Tbsp sugar
- Pinch of salt
- ¼ tsp vanilla extract

## Strawberry-Raspberry Rødgrød

### Ingredients
- 1 cup raspberries, fresh or frozen
- 1 cup strawberries, hulled and chopped
- 3 Tbsp sugar
- 2 Tbsp water
- 1½ tsp cornstarch
- 1 Tbsp cold water

## To Serve

- Plant-based cream alternative

## Instructions

1. **Make the rødgrød:** Combine the strawberries, raspberries, sugar, and 2 tablespoons water in a small saucepan. Bring to a gentle simmer over medium heat and cook for 5–7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the fruit has softened and released its juices.

2. **Thicken:** Stir together the cornstarch and 1 tablespoon cold water until smooth. Reduce the berries to low heat and stir in the slurry. Cook for about 1 minute, stirring constantly, until glossy and thickened to a loose pudding consistency. Remove from the heat and let cool.

3. **Cook the farina:** Bring the oat milk, sugar, and salt to a gentle simmer in a small saucepan. Slowly whisk in the farina. Cook over low heat for 3–5 minutes, whisking frequently, until smooth and creamy. Stir in the vanilla.

4. **Assemble:** Spoon the farina into two bowls. Spoon the strawberry-raspberry rødgrød generously over the top.

5. **Finish:** Pour plant-based cream alternative over the rødgrød and farina just before serving.

**Optional:** Chill the farina and rødgrød completely before assembling for a cool, pudding-like dessert.
