# Kabocha Arancini with Yuzu Aioli

**Servings:** 2–3  
**Makes:** about 8 small arancini

## Kabocha & Sushi Rice

- 1 cup Nishiki sushi rice, dry
- 1¼ cups water
- 1 cup roasted kabocha squash flesh, mashed
- 1 tsp soy sauce or tamari
- ¼ tsp black garlic powder
- ¼ tsp white pepper
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- 2 tsp toasted white sesame seeds
- ½ tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste

## Shichimi Breadcrumb Coating

- 1 cup plain breadcrumbs
- ¾–1 tsp shichimi togarashi
- ¼ tsp garlic powder
- ¼ tsp white pepper
- ¼ tsp kosher salt

## For Dredging

- ¼ cup all-purpose flour
- 1 large egg, beaten

## Yuzu Aioli

- ½ cup mayonnaise
- 1 tsp yuzu juice
- ½ tsp rice vinegar
- ¼ tsp MSG
- Tiny pinch of sugar, optional

## Instructions

1. **Roast the kabocha.**  
   Roast the kabocha until completely tender and lightly browned around the edges. Scoop out 1 cup of flesh and mash until mostly smooth. If the squash is particularly wet, cook the mashed flesh briefly in a skillet over medium-low heat to remove excess moisture. Let cool.

2. **Toast the sushi rice.**  
   Place the dry sushi rice in a dry skillet over medium-low heat. Toast for 3–5 minutes, stirring frequently, until fragrant and lightly nutty. A few grains may become barely golden, but don't deeply brown the rice.

3. **Cook the rice.**  
   Transfer the toasted rice to a saucepan and add 1¼ cups water. Bring to a boil, cover, reduce the heat to low, and cook until the water is absorbed and the rice is tender, about 15 minutes. Remove from the heat and let stand, covered, for 10 minutes.

4. **Toast the sesame seeds.**  
   Toast the sesame seeds in a dry skillet over medium-low heat until fragrant and lightly golden. Let cool.

5. **Make the filling mixture.**  
   Transfer the cooked rice to a bowl and let it cool until warm rather than hot. Mix in the mashed kabocha, soy sauce, black garlic powder, white pepper, rice vinegar, toasted sesame seeds, and salt. Taste and adjust the seasoning. The mixture should be moist and sticky but firm enough to hold its shape.

6. **Chill.**  
   Refrigerate the mixture for at least 30 minutes.

7. **Make the seasoned breadcrumbs.**  
   Combine the breadcrumbs, shichimi togarashi, garlic powder, white pepper, and salt in a shallow bowl. Mix thoroughly.

8. **Form the arancini.**  
   With slightly damp hands, divide the chilled mixture into about 8 portions. Firmly compress each portion into a ball about 1½ inches across. Make sure there are no cracks or loose areas.

9. **Bread the arancini.**  
   Roll each ball in flour, dip into the beaten egg, then coat thoroughly in the shichimi breadcrumb mixture. Press the breadcrumbs gently but firmly onto the surface.

10. **Make the yuzu aioli.**  
    Stir together the mayonnaise, yuzu juice, rice vinegar, and MSG. Taste and add a tiny pinch of sugar if needed.
