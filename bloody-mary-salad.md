# Bloody Mary Salad

Servings: 4

## Ingredients

### Salad
- 2 lb heirloom tomatoes, cut into bite-sized wedges or chunks
- ¼ cup Spanish queen olives, pitted and chopped
- ¼ cup fresh parsley, finely chopped
- Kosher salt, to taste
- Freshly cracked black pepper, to taste

### Dressing
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp lime juice
- 1 tsp olive brine
- 1 tsp Maggi seasoning
- 1 tsp Louisiana hot sauce
- ¼–½ tsp tomato paste, optional
- Celery salt, to taste

## Instructions

1. Place the tomatoes in a large bowl and lightly season with kosher salt. Let stand for about 5 minutes, allowing some tomato juice to accumulate.
2. Add the Spanish queen olives and parsley.
3. Whisk the olive oil, lemon juice, lime juice, olive brine, Maggi seasoning, Louisiana hot sauce, tomato paste, if using, and celery salt until well combined.
4. Pour the dressing over the tomatoes, including the accumulated tomato juice, and gently toss.
5. Season with freshly cracked black pepper and adjust the celery salt or other seasonings to taste.
6. Serve immediately.
